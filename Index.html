About Phoslab Labs Holding

Phoslab Labs Holding Company represents a strategic consolidation of specialized testing and services businesses serving the cannabis, environmental, botanical, and technology sectors. Our portfolio of complementary brands brings together deep industry expertise, advanced analytical capabilities, and innovative solutions.

Each of our brands operates as an independent business unit while benefiting from shared resources, collective expertise, and operational synergies across the holding company. This structure allows us to deliver specialized services with world-class quality while maintaining the agility and focus that each market demands.

Our unified mission is to provide accurate, dependable testing and services that help our clients navigate complex regulatory requirements, ensure product safety and quality, and optimize their operations across multiple industry verticals.
